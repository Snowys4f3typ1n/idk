Recipe Ingredients

A cozy homemade pizza for a family supper

  • Warm water: 1 cup (105–115°F)
  • Sugar: 1 tsp
  • Active dry yeast: 1 packet (approx. 2.25 tsp)
  • All-purpose or bread flour: 2.5 cups
  • Olive oil: 2 tbsp
  • Salt: 1 tsp

____________

  • Pizza sauce: 1/2 cup
  • Shredded mozzarella cheese: 1.5 cups
  • Grated parmesan cheese: 1/4 cup
  • Italian seasoning: 1.5 tsp

Steps

  1. Activate the Yeast:
    In a medium bowl, combine the warm water, sugar, and yeast. Let it sit for 5 to 10 minutes until it becomes froamy.

  2. Mix the Dough:
    Stir in the flour, olive oil, and salt until a smooth dough forms. Knead lightly by hand or with a mixer hook for about 5 minutes, then let it rest for 5 minutes.

  3. Preheat the Oven:
    Turn your oven up to 500°F (260°C). If you are using a specialized baking tool like a Pizza Stone or a preheated baking sheet, place it inside the oven now

  4. Shape the Crust:
    On a lightly floured surface, pat or roll the dough into a 12-inch circle. Transfer it onto a piece of parchment paper or a greased baking sheet.

  5. Assemble Top:
    Spread the pizza sauce evenly over the dough, leaving a small border for the crust. Sprinkle the mozzarella, parmesan, and Italian seasoning over the top.

  6. Bake:
    Bake for 10 to 15 minutes until the crust edges are a deep golden brown and the cheese is hot and bubbling. Let cool slightly before slicing.
